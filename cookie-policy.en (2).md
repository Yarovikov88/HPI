# HPI.EXPERT Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2025-08-20

This Cookie Policy ("Policy") explains what cookies are, which cookies and similar technologies we use on `https://hpi.expert` (the "Site"), for what purposes, and how you can manage your preferences.

Questions? Contact: support@hpi.expert.

## 1. What Are Cookies
Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit the Site. They help recognize your browser, remember settings, and improve the Site. Similar technologies include localStorage, sessionStorage, pixels, and tags.

## 2. Cookies We Use
- Strictly necessary: core Site functions (authentication, security, page loading). Cannot be disabled.
- Functional: remember preferences (language, settings) and enhance user experience.
- Analytics: help us understand usage (visits, performance) to improve the Service.
- Marketing: used for ad personalization and measurement (only with consent).

## 3. Legal Bases and Consent (GDPR/ePrivacy)
- Strictly necessary cookies are processed under legitimate interest or necessity to provide the Site/services.
- Functional, analytics, and marketing cookies are set only with your consent via the banner/preferences center. You can change your choice at any time.

### 3.1. Regional Consent Parameters
- CIS: Opt-in model for non-essential cookies (via “Accept all” or category toggles); strictly necessary are always active.
- MENA: We follow local PDPL requirements (e.g., UAE/KSA/Qatar/Bahrain). Non-essential cookies are set only after explicit consent via the banner/preferences center.

## 4. Managing Cookies
- Consent banner: on first visit, you can accept all, reject non-essentials, or customize.
- Preferences center: accessible via “Cookie settings” on the Site; change choices anytime.
- Browser settings: you can block/delete cookies. Some features may be affected.

## 5. Retention
- Session cookies are deleted when the browser closes.
- Persistent cookies last a set period (typically 1 day to 12 months) or until deleted.
Exact durations depend on provider and purpose.

## 6. Third-Party Cookies
We may use third-party analytics and/or marketing services that set their own cookies. Such providers operate under their own privacy policies. The list of providers may change.

## 7. International Transfers
Cookie processing may involve transfers to our processors (hosting, analytics, marketing) and, if needed, to other countries with appropriate safeguards (e.g., EU SCCs).

We may update this Policy. A new version will be posted with the date. Material changes may be accompanied by notice.
