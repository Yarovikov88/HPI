# Recommendation Technology at HPI.EXPERT

Last updated: 2025-08-20

This document explains how HPI.EXPERT generates personalized recommendations: what data is used, which algorithms are applied, how quality is evaluated, and what controls and rights you have.

## 1. Goals
- Increase awareness and progress across key life spheres.
- Detect blind spots and suggest next best actions.
- Support goal setting and achievement through metrics and tracking.

## 2. Data Used
- Survey/diagnostic answers by spheres.
- Goals, tasks, progress logs, achievements.
- Aggregated, anonymized patterns from users with similar trajectories.
- Technical signals (timestamps, interaction frequency).
Processing of special categories and personalization occurs only with your consent. See the Privacy Policy for details.

## 3. Algorithms and Logic
- Rules and heuristics: checklists, threshold triggers.
- Collaborative filtering: similar development trajectories.
- Personalized models: factor weighting tailored to your profile.
- Generative phrasing: clear, actionable text based on structured factors.
Recommendations are assistive and do not have legal or similarly significant effects without human involvement.

## 4. Quality and Validation
- A/B evaluation of wording and prioritization.
- Utility metrics: suggestion acceptance, subsequent progress on target metrics.
- Safety: harmful‑advice filters; disclaimers for medical/financial topics.

## 5. Personalization and Controls
- Toggle personalization in profile settings.
- Configure sphere priorities and suggestion types.
- Dismiss/mark irrelevant recommendations to improve future results.

## 6. Transparency
- “Why am I seeing this?” with key contributing factors (e.g., low activity in sphere X for N days).
- Links to related metrics and goals.

## 7. Limitations
- Quality depends on completeness and freshness of data.
- Algorithmic errors are possible; apply critical thinking.
- Not medical, legal, or financial advice.

## 8. Privacy and Security
- Data minimization; encryption in transit and at rest.
- Anonymization for aggregated model training.
- Compliance with GDPR and applicable local laws.
See also: Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.

## 9. Your Rights
- Access to a general explanation of logic and key factors.
- Right to object to profiling for marketing.
- Ability to opt out of personalized recommendations (may limit functionality).

## 10. Feedback
Contact support@hpi.expert — your feedback helps us improve recommendation quality.
