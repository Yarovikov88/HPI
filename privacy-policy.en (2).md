# HPI.EXPERT Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-08-20

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how HPI.EXPERT ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you use the website `https://hpi.expert` and related services (the "Service").

By using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. If you disagree, please do not use the Service.

## 1. Who We Are
Data controller: HPI.EXPERT rights holder (legal details to be added). Contact: support@hpi.expert.

## 2. Data We Process
We may process the following categories:
- Account data: name, email, password (hashed), interface language.
- Diagnostic data: answers across 8 life spheres, notes, goals, plans, progress.
- Technical data: IP address, device identifiers, cookies, logs, browser, OS.
- Payment data: tokens/IDs from payment providers (we do not store card numbers).
- Communications: support requests, feedback, survey responses.

Special categories (e.g., health) may be processed only with your explicit consent when you voluntarily provide such data for diagnostic purposes.

## 3. Purposes and Legal Bases (GDPR)
- Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)): registration, providing the Service, billing.
- Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): marketing emails, non-essential cookies, processing special categories.
- Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): improve the Service, prevent fraud, aggregated analytics.
- Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): accounting/tax compliance, lawful requests.

## 4. How We Use Data
- Provide functionality: HPI calculation, visualization, recommendations, goals, integrations.
- Personalization: adapt interface and recommendations to your profile.
- Analytics: aggregated statistics to improve the Service.
- Communications: service notices, support replies, marketing with consent.
- Security: incident monitoring and abuse prevention.

## 5. AI Processing and Automated Decisions
We use AI to produce recommendations and identify correlations between spheres. These are assistive and do not produce legal or similarly significant effects without human involvement. You may:
- request a general explanation of the logic;
- object to profiling for marketing;
- opt out of personalized recommendations (may limit functionality).

## 6. Cookies and Similar Technologies
We use cookies for:
- strictly necessary purposes (auth, security);
- preferences (language, settings);
- analytics (page visits, performance);
- marketing (with consent).

Manage cookies via the banner and browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect functionality. See our Cookie Policy (`/en/cookies`).

## 7. Retention
- Account/diagnostic data: while your account is active and up to 3 years after last activity, for reporting and legal protection.
- Security logs: up to 12 months.
- Transaction records: up to 5 years or longer if required by law.
After retention, data is deleted or anonymized.

## 8. Sharing with Third Parties
We may share data with processors under contracts and only as needed to provide the Service, e.g.:
- payment providers;
- hosting/cloud providers;
- analytics/monitoring providers;
- email/support providers.
We do not sell your personal data.

## 9. International Transfers
Where data is transferred outside your jurisdiction, we implement appropriate safeguards (e.g., EU SCCs). Details are available on request.

## 9.1. Regional Specifics and Localization
- CIS: We comply with applicable national laws. For users in the Russian Federation, we consider Federal Law No. 152-FZ (Personal Data) and localization (Federal Law No. 242-FZ), which may require primary databases in Russia and safeguards for cross-border transfers.
- MENA: For users in MENA (e.g., UAE PDPL, KSA PDPL, Bahrain PDPL, Qatar PDPL), we comply with local requirements on processing and cross-border transfers, including contractual safeguards and, where needed, risk assessments.
- GDPR: GDPR applies when we serve EEA residents or otherwise fall under GDPR. For other regions, we apply comparable standards of data protection and transparency.

## 10. Security
We apply measures including:
- encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (e.g., AES-256);
- access control, audit, environment segmentation;
- backups and incident monitoring.
No method is 100% secure.
